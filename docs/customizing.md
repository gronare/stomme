- [Theme a site](how-to/theme-a-site.md): colours, dark mode, fonts, your own CSS and theme directories.
- [Add a custom block](how-to/add-a-custom-block.md): a block of your own, and replacing or extending a built-in one.
- [Site config](reference/site-config.md): routes, locale, strings and every other key.
- [Translate a site](how-to/translate-a-site.md): the two locale fields and translated pages.
- [Package exports](reference/exports.md): every import path, `Cover.astro` and `renderMarkdown` for images.
